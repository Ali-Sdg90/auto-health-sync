In short: Auto Health Sync creates daily health summaries on your Android device and uploads them directly to your Google Drive. The developer does not receive or store your health data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Auto Health Sync Android application and its public website. Auto Health Sync is a direct-to-Drive backup utility, not a medical service, health dashboard, or developer-operated cloud storage service.

2. Data the app can access

With your explicit permission, Auto Health Sync can read these categories from Android Health Connect:

  • Steps and distance
  • Exercise sessions and duration
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂)
  • Weight

You choose which available categories are included in backups. The app requests background-read access so scheduled backups can run without opening the app, and history-read access so you can create a backup for an older date. It never writes to, modifies, or deletes Health Connect records.

3. How health data is used

Health data is used only to create the daily backup you request or schedule. On your device, the app reduces available records into a compact summary containing selected daily values such as totals, averages, minimums, maximums, and session durations.

Raw heart-rate samples, individual sleep-stage events, GPS points, exercise routes, and other high-frequency records are not placed in the backup. Auto Health Sync does not maintain a separate local health database.

4. Google Drive backup

The daily JSON summary is uploaded directly from your device to a folder created or reused by Auto Health Sync in your Google Drive. Existing daily files created by the app may be updated to prevent duplicates.

The app requests the limited Google Drive drive.file scope. This allows it to work with files it creates or that you explicitly make available to it; it does not grant broad access to unrelated files in your Drive. Auto Health Sync does not independently store Google OAuth access tokens.

Your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data stored on your device

To operate reliably, the app stores a small amount of local state, including:

  • Onboarding and setup completion
  • Your backup time, folder name, filename date format, and selected health categories
  • Google Drive folder and app-created file identifiers
  • Recent successful backup dates and the latest backup status
  • Up to 50 recent operational activity entries

Recent Activity contains status messages and dates, not raw health measurements. The app's local state is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

6. Data collection, sharing, and tracking

Auto Health Sync has no developer-operated backend, user-account system, Firebase integration, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDK. The developer does not receive, sell, rent, or use your health data for advertising.

Data is sent to Google Drive only when needed to provide the backup feature you enable. No health data is shared with the developer or unrelated third parties.

7. Retention, deletion, and your controls

Auto Health Sync does not create or maintain a developer-operated user account, so there is no separate app account to delete.

You remain in control of the data handled by the app:

  • Disable individual health categories in the app's Included data settings.
  • Revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • Revoke Google access from your Google Account connections.
  • Delete daily JSON files or the Auto Health Sync folder directly from Google Drive.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local settings and operational state.

Uninstalling the app does not delete files already stored in your Google Drive. Those user-owned files remain until you delete them.

8. Security

Auto Health Sync minimizes data exposure by processing summaries on the device, sending Google Drive API requests over HTTPS, requesting narrowly scoped Google Drive access, and avoiding developer-controlled storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free, but the app is designed to keep the data path short and under your control.

9. Children's privacy

Auto Health Sync is a personal utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app because the app does not send personal or health data to a developer-operated service.

10. Medical disclaimer

Auto Health Sync is intended only for personal data backup. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency monitoring, or medical-device functionality.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will remain available at this URL, and the date at the top of the page will be updated.
