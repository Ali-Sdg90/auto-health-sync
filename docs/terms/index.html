2. What Health Data Relay does

Health Data Relay reads the Health Connect categories you authorize, creates a compact daily JSON summary on your Android device, and uploads it directly to a folder in your Google Drive. It supports manual and scheduled backups, limited recent-day recovery, and user-selected report dates.

The app is provided without a developer-operated backend, independent user account, purchase, or subscription.
